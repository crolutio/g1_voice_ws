About Aideology:
- In one sentence: Aideology is an AI infrastructure company based in Dubai that helps organisations take their AI projects from pilot to full production, using GPU-accelerated computing and deep learning systems.
- Aideology (website aideology.ai, sometimes written AIdeology) is an AI infrastructure and enterprise technology company.
- It specialises in GPU-accelerated computing, deep learning systems and scalable AI deployment.
- It helps companies and public sector institutions move their AI projects from pilot stage into full-scale production.
- It deploys integrated AI software stacks and infrastructure for applications such as natural language processing, anomaly detection, computer vision, predictive analytics and intelligent automation.
- It designs custom AI architectures for industries such as healthcare, smart cities, telecommunications and government.
- It was founded in 2021 and is based in Dubai Silicon Oasis, in the United Arab Emirates.
- Its founder and CEO is Jorge Colotto.
- In October 2024, MDS System Integration Group (MDS SI) announced that it had agreed to buy a majority stake in Aideology.
